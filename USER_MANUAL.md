# StudentCenter — User Manual

## Getting Started

### Login
1. Go to `https://studentcenter.smkn2surakarta.sch.id/login`
2. Enter your school email and password
3. Click **Masuk Ke Student Center**

### Logout
Go to **Profil Saya** → click **Keluar Sesi**

---

## Features

### Mading Digital (Bulletin Board)
- Browse school announcements at `/mading`
- Filter by category: Semua, Olahraga, Sains & Teknologi, Seni & Budaya, Organisasi
- Search by keyword using the search bar
- Click any card to read full article

### Fasilitas (Facility Booking)
- Browse available facilities at `/fasilitas`
- Filter by type: Semua, Tempat, Peralatan
- Click a facility card → **Ajukan Peminjaman**
- Fill in purpose, date, and time → Submit
- Track booking status in the admin panel

### Proposal Kegiatan
- Go to `/proposal`
- Fill in activity details and attach proposal document URL
- Submit — proposal goes to OSIS → Guru → Admin approval chain
- Track status under **Proposal Saya**

### Ekstrakurikuler
- Browse all extracurriculars at `/ekstrakurikuler`
- Click to view members, schedule, and details

---

## Profile
- View your account info and role at `/profile`
- Your role determines access: Student, OSIS, Guru, Admin

---

## Troubleshooting

| Issue | Solution |
|---|---|
| Cannot login | Check email/password; contact admin if account locked |
| Proposal not showing | Refresh page; check network connection |
| Booking rejected | Check rejection reason in admin panel notification |
| Page not loading | Clear browser cache; try incognito mode |
